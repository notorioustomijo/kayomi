the work

One coaching business. Four places leads were quietly dying. Here's what fixed each one.

Every build starts the same way: walk through your actual day, find where a lead goes cold or a deal stalls, then build a system around that exact gap — not a generic bot bolted onto your business.

6–7 AM · Overnight backlog

Waking up to a ranked list, not a pile

0Leads sitting unread by morning
1stThing he sees is what actually matters

The gap

Every morning meant opening the phone to a backlog of DMs and podcast-lead opt-ins from overnight, with no way to tell which ones were actually hot before wasting time on the wrong one.

What I built

An overnight triage system that scores and sorts every inbound lead by intent while he sleeps, so his inbox opens to a ranked list instead of a pile — and the coldest inquiries never eat into his morning.
