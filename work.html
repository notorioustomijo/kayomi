the work

Four kinds of businesses. Four different bots. Same job — never miss a lead.

Every build starts the same way: figure out where your leads actually slip through, then design a chatbot or voicebot around that exact gap. Here's what that's looked like across a few different types of clients.

Salon & Beauty

An Instagram DM system that books itself

40sAvg. reply time
3xMore DMs converted to bookings

The gap

Bookings were coming in through Instagram DMs, but replies depended on whoever had a free hand — inquiries sat for hours and a lot of them just went cold.

What I built

A ManyChat flow that greets every DM instantly, asks the right qualifying questions (service, preferred date, budget), and drops a confirmed slot straight onto the calendar — with a human handoff built in for anything unusual.

Coaching

A lead-qualification flow that protects the calendar

60%Fewer unqualified calls booked
24/7Lead capture, no manual screening

The gap

Discovery calls were filling up with people who weren't a fit — budget, timeline, or goals didn't line up, and that was only becoming clear once the call had already started.

What I built

A conversational qualification flow across web chat and Instagram that filters serious leads before they ever reach the calendar, and routes the good-fit ones straight to a booking link.

0Missed calls after hours
100%Calls confirmed by SMS

The gap

Front-desk staff couldn't answer every call during peak hours, and after-hours calls simply went to voicemail — patients would call the next available clinic instead.

80%Of FAQs resolved without a human
