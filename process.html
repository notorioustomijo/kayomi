how it works

No tech for you to learn, no guessing on my end. Just a system that fits how you already sell.

Here's exactly what happens between our first conversation and the system going live — plus what keeps it running well after that.

01First call

We talk about where leads are slipping

No form to fill out alone — a short call where you walk me through your calendar, your team, and where deals tend to go quiet: DMs left unanswered, calls missed, bookings that stall halfway. I ask the questions; you don't need to know the tech.

02Day 2–3

I map the conversation

Before any building happens, I sketch out how the system should actually talk — the questions it asks, the tone it uses, and the exact point where it either books the call or hands off to you.

03Day 4–7

I build it around how you already sell

The follow-up and lead-capture system gets built in the right tool for the job — ManyChat, Voiceflow, Chatbase, or Vapi — then connected to your calendar and CRM through Make or Zapier, and tested against real-world messy inputs, not just the happy path.

04Launch

It runs quietly in the background

Live and connected to your existing calendar and CRM in weeks, not months, with a plain-English walkthrough so you know exactly what it's doing — no black box, nothing to approve line by line unless you want to.

05Ongoing

I stick around

Most clients keep me on month-to-month to monitor how conversations are actually landing, fix what isn't converting, and add new automation as the business grows. This is where a lot of systems fall apart after a vendor disappears — I try to make sure yours doesn't.

Questions coaches usually ask first

Will this make my business feel less personal?

No — that's the whole point of how it's built. The system handles the mechanical follow-up (the reminders, the re-asks, the "did you still want to book") so you stay the relationship-driven closer. It protects your style, it doesn't replace it.

Do I need to know how to code, or become "a tech person"?

No. Everything is built and configured on my end — you get a working system, not a project to finish.

How long does a build take?

Most systems go from our first conversation to live and tested in about a week. More involved voice or multi-channel builds can take a bit longer.

What if it needs changes after launch?

That's expected. I offer ongoing monthly support for clients who want the system monitored and refined as their business changes — most do, and it's usually where the real results show up.

Which channels can it work across?

LinkedIn and web inquiries, email, podcast/webinar opt-ins, and inbound phone calls — one connected system, wherever your leads are actually coming from.
