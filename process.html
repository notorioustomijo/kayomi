how it works

No code for you to touch, no guessing on my end. Just a bot that fits how your business actually runs.

Here's exactly what happens between the form you fill out and the bot going live — plus what keeps it running well after that.

01Day 1

You fill a short form

A few questions about your business, where your leads currently come from, and where they tend to go quiet — DMs left unanswered, calls missed, bookings that stall halfway.

02Day 2–3

I map the conversation

Before any building happens, I sketch out how the bot should actually talk — the questions it asks, the tone it uses, and the exact point where it either books the appointment or hands off to you.

03Day 4–7

I build and test it

The chatbot or voicebot gets built in the right tool for the job — ManyChat, Voiceflow, Chatbase, or Vapi — then connected to your calendar and CRM through Make or Zapier, and tested against real-world messy inputs, not just the happy path.

04Launch day

You get a bot ready to plug in

Fully functional, connected to your existing pages and calendar, with a walkthrough so you know exactly how it works — no black box.

05Ongoing

I stick around

Most clients keep me on month-to-month to monitor how conversations are actually going, fix what isn't converting, and add new automations as the business grows. This part is where a lot of bots fall apart — I try to make sure yours doesn't.

Questions people usually ask first

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything is built and configured on my end — you get a working bot, not a project to finish.

How long does a build take?

Most projects go from form to a live, tested bot in about a week. More complex voicebot or multi-channel builds can take a bit longer.

What if it needs changes after launch?

That's expected. I offer ongoing monthly support for clients who want the bot monitored and refined as their business changes — most do.

Which platforms can it work on?

Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, your website, and inbound phone calls — one bot, or several, depending on where your leads actually are.
